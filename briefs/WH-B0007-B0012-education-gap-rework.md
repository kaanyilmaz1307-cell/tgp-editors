## Why this exists

Youssef's feedback on B0001 V1:

1. All eight ads used the same hooks and the same B-roll. That is one variation, not six.
2. The ask was 5–6 videos, each with a completely different clickbaity opening and completely different clips, each with 3–4 hooks (roughly 6 × 4 = 24 ads).
3. Script logic stays the same as the competitor. The only part the variants may share is the final product presentation.

Fix: six separate ClickUp tasks, one per visual world. Each has its own opening shot list, four fresh hooks, its own intro line, and its own clips under the first fact list. The product block is cut once and reused.

## B0007 — Kitchen Experiment Kid

**Opening clips (0–8s)**
1. ECU: kid's hands drop baking soda into red vinegar, foam erupts (volcano).
2. Kid shines a phone flashlight through a glass of water, rainbow on the wall, eyes go wide.
3. Kid rolls a peeled "rubber egg" across the table, it bounces.
4. Kid slices an apple, one half in lemon water, one plain. Cut to the plain half brown.
5. Kid looks up at camera grinning with the foaming glass. Freeze as the hook lands.

**Hooks**
- H1 (control, carried from B0001): "So, this is what they mean by the education gap."
- H2: "Your kid can do this experiment tonight. Most parents can't explain why it works."
- H3: "Watch what happens when a 9-year-old finally understands why."
- H4: "The smartest kid in class isn't the one with the tutor."

**Intro:** "While most kids are scrolling after dinner, kids who read encyclopedias already know:"

**Body clips (tree rings · rainbows · volcanoes · rivers):** kid counts rings on a log slice with a marker · glass-of-water rainbow slides as the flashlight moves · clay-mountain baking-soda volcano, overhead · water poured down a tilted sand tray carves a channel.

## B0008 — Screen vs Page Split

**Opening clips (0–8s)**
1. Split opens: left, kid on the sofa scrolling, blue light. Right, kid at a desk opening a book. Same angle.
2. Left overlay ticks "1h 12m… 2h 40m… 4h 05m". Right ticks "page 1… page 2".
3. Left: yawn, glazed eyes, thumb never stops. Right: kid points at a picture, turns to ask.
4. Split slides away, desk kid fills the frame, looks up. Hook lands.

**Hooks**
- H1: "Two kids. Same age. Same school. Watch the difference."
- H2: "Four hours on a screen, or two pages of this."
- H3: "Parents, this is what screen time is quietly costing your kid."
- H4: "Your kid's screen time report is the education gap."

**Intro:** "While your kids are glued to a screen after school, kids who read encyclopedias already know:"

**Body clips:** keep the split. Right side shows the fact, left side keeps scrolling. Log rings highlighted one by one · rainbow over a suburban street, kid at a window · lava close-up then cracked ground · drone follow of a river, then the scrolling kid looks up and the split closes.

## B0009 — Classroom Hand Up

**Opening clips (0–8s)**
1. Back-of-class: teacher writes "Why do rainbows appear?" and turns. Silence.
2. One hand shoots up in the front row. Slow motion, push in.
3. Two kids in the back exchange a look.
4. Front-row kid answers with confidence, teacher nods.
5. Whip pan to the kid turning to camera. Hook lands.

**Hooks**
- H1: "Ever notice it's always the same kid with their hand up?"
- H2: "Teachers can spot this kid in the first week of school."
- H3: "One kid in every class answers before the teacher finishes the question. Here's why."
- H4: "This is the kid the whole class copies from."

**Intro:** "While your kids are busy with cartoons and video games, kids who read encyclopedias already know:"

**Body clips:** teacher holds up a log slice, kid counts rings aloud · chalkboard raindrop drawing, kid draws the arc · desk model volcano erupts, kids lean in · wall map, kid traces the river to the sea.

## B0011 — Dinner Table Why

**Opening clips (0–8s)**
1. Handheld dinner table, kid mid-chew: "Why do we need to sleep?" (on-screen text).
2. Dad freezes, fork halfway. Freeze frame, zoom, record scratch.
3. Mom looks at dad. Dad checks his phone under the table. Text: "…ask Google?"
4. Kid waiting, chin on hands, eyebrows up. Hook lands.

**Hooks**
- H1: "Every parent freezes at this question."
- H2: "Your kid asks 'why' all day long. Here's what smart parents do with that."
- H3: "When your kid asks why, this is the wrong answer."
- H4: "Stop googling your kid's questions. Do this instead."

**Intro:** "While most parents answer with 'I don't know, ask Google,' kids who read encyclopedias already know:"

**Body clips:** phone-shot family moments. Kid and grandpa at a fresh stump · rainbow from the back seat · kid turns from a volcano documentary on TV · family on a bridge, kid asks where the water goes.

## B0012 — Report Card Reveal

**Opening clips (0–8s)**
1. ECU: red pen circles "A" on a science test. Hard cut in.
2. Parent's hands pull a report card from an envelope, pause on the science line.
3. Two papers slide in side by side: "A" and "C". Same test, same class.
4. The A kid at the table reading a book, casual, finger on the page. Hook lands.

**Hooks**
- H1: "The kid who got the highest score in science didn't study more."
- H2: "This is the difference between an A student and a C student. It isn't tutoring."
- H3: "Parents, look at this before the next report card comes home."
- H4: "Second grade is where the gap starts. Most parents notice in fifth."

**Intro:** "While your kids are watching videos all day, kids who read encyclopedias already know:"

**Body clips:** each fact as a test question answered in the kid's handwriting with a red tick. Ring sketch · rainbow answer, kid smiles at the paper · labelled volcano cross-section · river drawn mountain to sea, then the pen writes "A" at the top.
