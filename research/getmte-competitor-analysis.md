# MTE (getmte.com) — Best-Selling Competitor Map

**Date:** 2026-09-16
**Purpose:** Identify the highest-volume competitors to MTE ("More Than Energy") so the creative team knows whose ads to study, swipe, and counter-position against.
**Method:** Web research (press, funding databases, third-party ad-library trackers). Meta Ads Library and getmte.com were not reachable from the research sandbox, so ad counts below come from third-party trackers and are dated. Re-verify counts in the Ads Library before building briefs on them.

---

## 1. What MTE is (so we compare like with like)

| Attribute | MTE |
|---|---|
| Product | Caffeine-free "healthy energy + daily wellness" drink powder (stick packs and 30-serving bag); also a 2 oz shot (launched Sept 2022) |
| Active | Paraxanthine (caffeine's primary metabolite) + 9 other actives: ashwagandha, saffron, nootropics, adaptogens |
| Claims | Steady energy, focus, mood, productivity. No jitters, no crash, no sleep disruption. Zero sugar, no sucralose, no artificial ingredients |
| Price | 24-pack: $3.25/serving one-time, $2.60/serving on subscription. 30-serving bag: $70 one-time / $60 sub ($2.33 / $1.99 per drink) |
| Model | DTC subscription first, Amazon second (Amazon listing: 88 reviews, 4.2 stars as of search date). Site reviews: ~1,100+ via Loox, 4.7 avg |
| Founder / face | Jeff Boyd (finance background, exited a shipping company 2019). Reggie Watts partnership and Huckleberry Açaí flavor (Forbes, June 2026) |
| Scale signals | Forbes cites a ~50% repeat purchase rate. No revenue figure is public. Amazon review count suggests MTE is still a small DTC brand relative to the list below |
| Conquest targets MTE itself names in blog comparisons | MUD\WTR, Ethan's, Elevated Energy, Wake Up Water, Supergreen Tonik |

**Competitive frame:** MTE sells the *morning ritual / clean-energy powder* job, with a caffeine-free twist. Its real competition is not Red Bull. It is the DTC subscription powders and shots that run heavy Meta UGC, and the new paraxanthine RTD brands taking the "caffeine-free energy" claim mainstream.

## 3. What this means for creative (recommendation)

1. **Swipe structure from RYZE, message from Ketone-IQ, format from Magic Mind.** RYZE has the highest-volume, most-tested ad architecture (problem → coffee-anxiety confession → ingredient explainer → offer). Ketone-IQ has the only proven caffeine-free-energy ad language at nine-figure scale. Magic Mind is the closest shot-format rival.
2. **Counter-position against UPDATE now, before Q4.** UPDATE will spend against "paraxanthine, no crash" with Kim Kardashian. MTE cannot out-spend that. The defensible angle is *paraxanthine + 9 adaptogens/nootropics, no sugar, no sucralose, no can*: "UPDATE is the energy drink version; MTE is the daily wellness version." Build a "paraxanthine, but complete" comparison ad set.
3. **Price anchor is a problem.** MTE at $2.60–3.25 per serving is 2–3x RYZE / Everyday Dose / MUD\WTR. Ads must sell it as a *replacement for the whole supplement stack* (energy + mood + focus + adaptogens), not as a coffee alternative, or the price comparison kills conversion.
4. **Repeat rate is the story.** Forbes' ~50% repeat purchase figure is a stronger hook than any ingredient claim. Use it in retention-style creative ("why half of MTE customers reorder").
